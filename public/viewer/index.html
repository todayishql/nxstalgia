Hall of Fame

How the Hall of Fame works
The region pills re-run the whole ranking inside one region, so you get the top 100 of that region — ranks, "+/-" and the 100-song cut are all recomputed against that region alone. A song's region comes from its artists (tagged in /admin/artists); a collaboration across regions counts in each of them, and "Untagged" collects songs whose artists have no region yet. The Hall of Fame shows the top 100 songs on whichever column you're sorted by — click Pre-chart / On-chart / All-time to switch (click again to flip direction); each click re-picks the top 100 for that exact metric, not just re-orders the all-time 100. "#" is the rank on the column you sorted by. "Pre-chart" = total streams accumulated before the chart existed (before 2026). "+/-" = rank shift from the pre-chart ranking to the all-time ranking (how far on-chart streams moved the song), regardless of sort; NEW = the song sat outside the pre-chart top 100 and only reached the all-time top 100 thanks to its on-chart streams. Season columns appear once there is more than one season of data.
